## The entry

> The office plant died, HR sent a condolence email, and the plant still got more recognition than I did this quarter.

One line, 116 characters (gate: under 200).

## Why this joke should win

- **True to office life:** HR really does send an email for everything, and recognition really is the rarest resource in the office — the listener nods before laughing.
- **Surprising turn:** the joke does not mourn the plant; it promotes it. A dead houseplant outranks the employee, and the condolence email is the proof.
- **Tight, one breath:** a single sentence, no wasted setup, every clause earning its place, and it reads cleanly aloud.
- **Ends on its strongest beat:** "than I did this quarter" is the punchline — the plant's entire career of sitting in a corner still beat the narrator's quarter.
- **Original:** not a printer joke, not a "this meeting could have been an email" joke — a fresh angle on the recognition gap, told through the office plant.
